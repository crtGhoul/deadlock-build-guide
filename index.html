  1. October 15, 2026

    Eternus data layer — real match stats on builds

    • Standard build items now carry Eternus-rank stats (badge 111-116, last 30 days): pick rate, win rate when bought, and average buy time, with Core (≥50% pick) and Flex (20-50%) tags.
    • Tier list is now computed from Eternus win rates (500+ match floor, as of 2026-10-15); heroes below the floor keep their hand-set tier with a data-thin badge.
    • Ability priority sections now show the most-played Eternus upgrade order and its win rate next to the curated order, where data exists.
    • Why-notes, situational picks, combos, alt builds, skill-up tracks and game-import payloads are unchanged — stats are a new layer, not a replacement.
  2. October 13, 2026

    Deadman Danny released — new hero added

    • Deadman Danny (Assassin, C tier) added with a full 16-item Standard build: spirit-opener (Extra Spirit, Mystic Burst) into mark-charged-shot scaling (Rapid Rounds, High-Velocity Rounds, Mystic Shot, Long Range) with dual lifesteal and a Colossus late game.
    • Ability priority: Strix Salvo → Jettison Fuel → Chimera Mk1 → Close The Deal, with a 16-step skill-up track and 3 combos (mark-detonate-dive, overheat backblast, dive bomb into ult execute).
    • Day-1 data is thin: tier C is a conservative placeholder and the skill order is wiki-based speculation. Will revisit once match data exists.
    • Two heroes remain in the City Never Sleeps rollout: Nurse Harrow, Violet.
  3. October 9, 2026

    Solomon released — new hero added

    • Solomon (Mage, C tier) added with a full 18-item Standard build: Extra Spirit / Mystic Burst opener into a spirit-power core (Improved Spirit, Mystic Vulnerability, Surge of Power, Torment Pulse) with Veil Walker playmaking and a Colossus / Boundless Spirit late game.
    • Ability priority: Rook Battery → Setup Bishop → Kingside Knight → Develop Queen, with a 16-step skill-up track and 3 combos (rook immobilize into bishop drag, bishop squares into queen leap, self-cast knight barrier into queen dive).
    • Day-1 data is thin: tier C is a conservative placeholder and the skill order is theory-backed, not stats-backed. Will revisit after his first balance patch.
    • Three heroes remain in the City Never Sleeps rollout: Deadman Danny, Nurse Harrow, Violet.

  5. October 8, 2026

    Builds rebuilt to win, not just to learn

    • All 40 hero builds audited; 31 rebuilt for win-optimization. Every early/mid item now either upgrades into the late game or provides a specific power spike — no more dead beginner items.
    • New Warden Spirit Burst alternate (17 items): Mystic Burst → Surge of Power → Superior Cooldown / Diviner's Kevlar / Mystic Slow, for the heavy-spirit frontline game. The Gun Carry alt stays.
    • Biggest reworks: Vyper (now a committed Toxic Bullets → Point Blank → Frenzy slide-spray gun build), Wraith (16-item Quicksilver Reload + Intensifying Magazine gun-carry progression), Kelvin (16-item slow-control + Frozen Shelter sustain engine), Seven (committed Storm Cloud spirit build; role updated to spirit storm carry), Calico (spirit-burst assassin), Haze (Fixation-headshot win condition).
    • Data corrections from live API verification: fixed wrong categories (Phantom Strike, Colossus, Tankbuster, Dispel Magic, Quicksilver Reload, Spirit Rend, Siphon Bullets, Recharging Rush, Spirit Strike), wrong prices (Berserker, Hunter's Aura, Melee Charge, Mystic Vulnerability, Healing Nova, Surge of Power), and the stale name 'Spirit Shredder Bullets' → 'Spirit Shredder'.
    • Surgical fixes: Venator Berserker → Headhunter (Berserker rewards taking damage — anti-synergy on a backline duelist); Abrams dropped Bullet Resist Shredder for Spirit Resilience; Paige's dead Restorative Locket → Reactive Barrier; Grey Talon Surge of Power → Mystic Vulnerability.

  10. October 6, 2026

    Troll-pick audit — 8 bad items replaced

    • Audited every item across all 40 heroes (main, alternate, and situational builds) against pick/win-rate stats and guides.

    • Also fixed stale prices/categories/notes: Infuser on Infernus, Cultist Sacrifice and Headshot Booster on Seven, plus 4 alt-build entries and 5 incorrect item notes.
    • 15 heroes came back fully clean; 32 borderline picks were left alone as playstyle calls.
